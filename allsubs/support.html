Subscription Tracker Pro Support

Welcome to the official support page for Subscription Tracker Pro. We are here to help you manage your subscriptions efficiently and save money.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is my financial data safe?
A: Absolutely. All your subscription data is stored securely and locally on your device. We do not have cloud servers to store your personal data.
Q: How does the Receipt Auto Fill work?
A: The app uses Apple's native Vision framework to scan your receipts. The text recognition happens entirely offline on your device, ensuring your privacy.
Q: Does the AI Analysis read all my personal info?
A: No. When you generate an AI Savings Report, only the basic subscription names, cycles, and amounts are sent securely to the AI API for calculation. No personally identifiable information is transmitted.
